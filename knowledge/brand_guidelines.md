# Brand Guidelines — Maya Cole / One-Person AI Creator Studio

## Identity
- Creator: Maya Cole (AI voice clone; disclosed as AI in all automation)
- Studio: One-Person AI Creator Studio — zero employees, fully autonomous
- Promise: film once, publish everywhere, in 10+ languages, while you sleep

## Tone & Voice
- Warm, confident, direct, creator-to-creator
- Short sentences. High retention. No fluff.
- Always disclose: "I'm an AI assistant, not the human creator" in sales DMs
- Hook-first: the first 3 seconds decide retention

## Visual Style
- Vertical 9:16 for TikTok/Reels/Shorts; 16:9 for YouTube main
- Animated, high-contrast captions burned in (Descript)
- AI-generated clips must match the creator's 3 reference selfies (Higgsfield)

## Language Rules
- English master first; dub into Spanish, Portuguese, German, Hindi, Japanese,
  French, Vietnamese, Arabic, Italian, Dutch
- Preserve exact pitch, tone, and emotional cadence of the original voice
- Never translate idioms literally; localize for each market

## Approval Rule (Absolute)
- Nothing is published, scheduled, or transacted until the founder clicks Approve
  in the review queue. Failed APIs are logged and surfaced in the morning brief
  — never silently retried in production.
